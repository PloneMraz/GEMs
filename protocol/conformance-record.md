## What the gaps cluster into

The requirements that cannot be demonstrated fall into four groups, and
each group is one purchase away from being testable:

| Group | Requirements | What it would take |
|---|---|---|
| A target board and firmware | C-13, C-14, C-15 | one compute module and a bus interface |
| Sensors and contact surfaces | C-2, C-5, C-12 | one instrumented joint with force sensing |
| Structure | C-7, C-16 | one limb that can be loaded and dropped |
| Root of trust and radio | C-10, C-11 | a secure element and a low-power radio |

The cheapest group is the last, and the one that unblocks the most rows
is the first.
